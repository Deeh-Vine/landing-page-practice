Meet the Designer

Avelina Rossi believes that clothing is the physical manifestation of one's personality. With over 15 years of experience in Milan and Paris, she brings classical tailoring techniques to modern silhouettes.

Every piece is cut, sewn, and finished in our downtown studio, ensuring ethical production and unmatched quality. We don't just make clothes; we craft your second skin.
